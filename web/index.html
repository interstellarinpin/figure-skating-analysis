The falling cat: turning with nothing to spin on

Here L = 0 the whole time — no spin is borrowed. The two halves bend and counter-twist around one closed loop in shape space (right), and the body still nets a real rotation (left). That leftover turn is the geometric phase, and it is the snap, isolated.
